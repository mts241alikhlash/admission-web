New menu Verifikasi Berkas for staff with `admission-documents.read`: a queue of applicants (Menunggu, Perlu perbaikan, Selesai) and a review page where each document is previewed in a popup, approved or rejected silently, and the result is sent to the applicant once with Kirim hasil, or the form is returned with Minta perbaikan data. Detail Pendaftar shows its document, revision and verify buttons only with `admission-documents.verify`.
